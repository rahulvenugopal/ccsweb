Four decades, one question

1976

Project Consciousness

Inaugurated at NIMHANS (Dept. of Neurophysiology, ICMR-funded under Prof. T. Desiraju). A whole-body study of yoga and meditation in sound-proof cabins: 91 men registered; TM, Rajayoga, Yoga Nidra, pranayama.

1982–85

Symposium and first lab

A 1982 symposium on Yoga, Brain & Consciousness brought yoga, neuroscience and philosophy together; the first lab followed with a 13-channel EEG polygraph, a 4-channel portable polygraph and Russian collaborators.

2000–2020

Cognitive & Sleep Lab

Under Prof. Bindu M. Kutty. Expanded beyond sleep into high-density EEG/ERP, fMRI, cognitive paradigm development, and meditation studies across traditions, alongside whole-night polysomnography, Ayurveda/yoga trials, schizophrenia, and lucid dream decoding.

2020+

Centre for Consciousness Studies

Founded by Prof. Bindu M. Kutty. Four core areas: sleep & dreams, mind–body & contemplative, clinical & developmental, neurotechnology. Cross-tradition EEG, wearables, open-source tools.

Yoga is not a relaxation switch. It trains homeostatic adaptability. Lesson of Project Consciousness: after TM, ventilation fell; after Rajayoga and pranayama it rose. Every person showed their own pattern, session to session.
