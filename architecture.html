Four decades, one question

1976

Project Consciousness

Inaugurated at NIMHANS (Dept. of Neurophysiology, ICMR-funded under Prof. T. Desiraju). A whole-body study of yoga and meditation in sound-proof cabins: 91 men registered; TM, Rajayoga, Yoga Nidra, pranayama.

1982–85

Symposium and first lab

A 1982 symposium on Yoga, Brain & Consciousness brought yoga, neuroscience and philosophy together; the first lab followed with a 13-channel EEG polygraph, a 4-channel portable polygraph and Russian collaborators.

2000–2020

Cognitive & Sleep Lab

Whole-night polysomnography as a window on consciousness: meditators’ sleep, Ayurveda and yoga trials, schizophrenia, lucid dreaming, dream decoding.

2020+

Centre for Consciousness Studies

Four core areas: sleep & dreams, mind–body & contemplative, clinical & developmental, neurotechnology. Cross-tradition EEG, wearables, open-source tools.

Yoga is not a relaxation switch. It trains homeostatic adaptability. Lesson of Project Consciousness: after TM, ventilation fell; after Rajayoga and pranayama it rose. Every person showed their own pattern, session to session.

No single “relaxation response”

Tidal volume and ventilation fell after TM but rose after Rajayoga and pranayama; lactate and cortisol fell in some seniors and rose in others. Effects read as adaptive optimisation across repeated sessions. CCS now records repeated, multi-day sessions and always includes rest and task conditions.

NIMHANS–ICMR Project Consciousness report (Desiraju)

60patients, RCT

Yoga added to migraine care raised vagal tone

Five days a week for six weeks: a greater reduction in headache frequency and intensity, with more vagal and less sympathetic activity. Hypothesis in progress: practice trains a loop, not a single organ.

Kisan et al., Int J Yoga 2014
Awarenessinside sleep

Sleep is more than disconnection: its responsiveness can be measured

The same tones evoke stage-dependent ERP waves (P200, N350, N550–P900) awake and in NREM sleep. Meditators show more dynamic NREM and REM patterns, and larger N550–P900 complexes correlate with fewer NREM disruptions, so K-complexes may be a protective gate against external sounds.

Venugopal et al., 2018 (n = 12 healthy volunteers); Venugopal et al., 2019
r = −0.49

A quieter heart signal in REM predicts a better morningpreliminary

Lower heart-evoked potential amplitude (300–400 ms after the R-peak) during REM sleep goes with higher mood ratings after waking (p = 0.047): reduced cortical processing of cardiac signals may reflect less self-referential, interoceptive processing, a link between sleep-based interoception and next-day mood.

CCS sleep study; full reference to be added

Schizophrenia: altered coordination across states

Whole-night sleep in patients shows more and longer awakenings, reduced N2, and unstable sleep cycles. Elevated serum adenosine deaminase in antipsychotic-naïve patients. Task EEG and fMRI show shared coordination differences with near-normal performance.

Sasidharan et al., Sleep Medicine 2017; Asian J Psychiatry 2017; PhD thesis 2015

72patients

Ayurveda for anxiety and sleep, objectively tested

In generalised anxiety, Manasamitra Vataka ± Shirodhara preserved slow-wave sleep and continuity, whereas clonazepam reduced N3. Whole-night polysomnography adds information beyond symptom ratings.

Tubaki et al., Current Science 2016
5/6vs 1/5

Reading and gently shaping dreams

Five of six participants trained in lucid dreaming (reality testing, autosuggestion, intention plus a REM auditory cue) achieved it, against one of five controls, with higher fronto-central gamma. In 29 healthy high-recallers, recall tracked beta power and default-mode connectivity; 10.4% of sleep cues surfaced in the next dream report. Not dream decoding.

Kumar et al., Int J Dream Research 2018; Consciousness & Cognition 2025
1404 × 35, triple-blind

Music reorganises engagement

Ten minutes of bansuri: alpha fell in every group, most in the control; frontal beta1 depended on the raga. Some ragas also gave smaller responses to unrelated sounds.

Kunikullaya et al., IJPP 2025; Music & Medicine 2022, 2025

When inner and outer clocks converge

Intrinsic neural timescales are longer for internal (breath) than external attention. Advanced meditators show no gap, and the smaller the gap, the stronger the reported non-duality (p = .02, 53 paired observations).

Saketh et al., Communications Biology 2026
2–3min to onset

Meditation is a trajectory, not a snapshot

EEG changes begin within 2–3 minutes and peak at 7–10. Long-term meditators switch between rest and meditation within a minute on demand; controls do not.

Malipeddi et al., 2025; Nair et al., Consciousness & Cognition 2017
51.6%of variance

Wellbeing follows practice proficiency

Across 1,009 Brahma Kumaris practitioners, proficiency predicted wellbeing better than years of practice. Isha practitioners (n = 1,352) report lower stress than controls, with a dose–response to practice; proficient Heartfulness meditators score higher on wellbeing.

Nair et al., IJW 2018; Frontiers in Public Health 2024; Sylapan et al., Consciousness & Cognition 2020
42 · 33 · 28advanced · novice · control

Advanced Isha practitioners sustain power in Shoonya

With 128-channel EEG, oscillatory power was sustained in advanced practitioners while novices and controls dropped; they also reported deeper meditation and non-duality. A hypothesis to test, not a result: flexible updating with less rigid identification.

Malipeddi et al., Mindfulness 2024 (cross-sectional)
